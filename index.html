Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2, 2026

Work Time Tracker ("the App") is a work time tracking application developed to help users record working hours, work-related travel, and information about work objects.

1. Information We Collect

Work Time Tracker does not collect personal information from users.

The App does not require users to create an account or provide their name, email address, telephone number, address, or other personal information.

Work-related information entered by the user, such as working hours, dates, work objects, travel records, notes, and related settings, is stored locally on the user's device.

2. How We Use Your Information

The information entered into the App is used only to provide the App's functionality, including recording working hours, keeping a history of work records, recording work-related travel, managing work objects, calculating work-related amounts, and importing and exporting the user's data.

3. Data Storage

The App stores user-entered work information locally on the user's device.

The developer does not operate a server that receives or stores this information.

4. Data Sharing

Work Time Tracker does not sell, rent, or share users' personal or work-related information with third parties.

The App does not use advertising networks or third-party analytics services to collect user information.

5. Google Play Billing

The App may offer optional paid features through Google Play Billing. Payments and purchase processing are handled by Google Play.

Work Time Tracker does not receive or store users' payment card numbers, bank account information, or other payment credentials.

6. Data Export and Import

The App may allow users to export their locally stored data, for example in JSON format, and import previously exported data.

Exported data is controlled by the user. The developer does not automatically receive exported files.

7. Data Security

The App stores work-related information locally on the user's device. Users are responsible for maintaining the security of their device and any exported files.

8. Children's Privacy

Work Time Tracker is intended for adults and workplace use. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the App's functionality or applicable requirements change.

10. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or the privacy practices of Work Time Tracker, you may contact the developer through the contact information provided on the App's Google Play store listing.

Work Time Tracker
